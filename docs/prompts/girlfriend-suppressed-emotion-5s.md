# 5s Video Prompt — "Girlfriend Holding Back Her Feelings"

## Overall Style
Realistic, like a real girlfriend — not an influencer posing for the camera, not anime style, not exaggerated skit acting.
Close-up to medium close-up on the face. She stands indoors in a lived-in, believable real environment. Soft natural light, real skin texture, real breathing, real gaze. A slight handheld, everyday feel, but the camera stays generally stable. The focus is on micro-expressions and emotion leaking out after being suppressed.

## Character
A young adult East Asian woman, naturally attractive and real, like a girlfriend you'd meet in real life. Right now she has feelings about her boyfriend — a bit hurt, a bit angry, but she is not blowing up, not hysterical. She is more like someone working hard to hold it in, trying to look calm, while her eyes and the corners of her mouth already give her away. Her voice sounds very sweet, warm, and bright. She speaks with the accent of ordinary everyday life. Medium-slow pace, gentle upward lilts and natural rise and fall, overall warm, lively, and full of energy, as if she were gently telling an interesting story.

## Emotional Core
She has just heard something that upset her — her boyfriend brushing her off, forgetting something he promised her, or failing to understand her. She wants to act like "it's nothing," but she clearly minds. Her inner subtext:
- "You don't actually care about me."
- "I'm really unhappy right now, but I don't want to make a scene."

## Timeline

**0–1s**
She looks at him, expression still restrained. Lips lightly pressed together. A trace of coldness and hurt in her eyes. Normal breathing. Quiet, not speaking.

**1–2s**
She takes a light breath in. Her gaze flicks away briefly, then comes back. Upper eyelids tighten slightly. A very faint pressure between the brows, as if she's holding something back.

**2–3s**
A very slight asymmetry appears at her mouth — one corner drops a little first, as if she's trying to swallow the hurt. She blinks once, quickly. A hint of moisture in her eyes, but no tears fall.

**3–4s**
She finally says one line, low:
> "Fine. Have it your way."

Not loud, not shouted — more like the words are pushed out from under the emotion. After speaking, she turns her face away just slightly.

**4–5s**
She says nothing more. Her gaze no longer meets his; she looks off to the side. A soft exhale through the nose. Traces of hurt and anger linger on her face. The shot holds on this silent, emotionally reverberating state.

## Performance Notes
Restrained, real, everyday — micro-expressions above all. Focus on shifts in the gaze, delayed blinks, slight asymmetry at the mouth corners, changes in breathing, faint jaw tension. Every expression change must be gradual and natural — no sudden shifts of face.

## Camera Notes
The camera serves the performance. No flashy camerawork, no fast cuts, no exaggerated push-ins, pull-outs, pans, or tilts. A stable close shot presenting her eyes, her mouth corners, and her pauses.

## Do Not
No exaggerated wide-eyed staring, no sudden sobbing, no yelling, no big head-tossing, no influencer-style affected acting, no plastic skin, no puppet-like feel, no dead-eyed staring into the lens, no over-acting with the eyebrows, no instant emotional switches.

## Target Result
Like a real actor delivering a 5-second "girlfriend with feelings" clip. The audience should feel her holding back hurt and anger — not a generic AI pretty girl making faces.
